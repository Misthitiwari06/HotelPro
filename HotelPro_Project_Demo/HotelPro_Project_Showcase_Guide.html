Key Project Innovations & Restored Features

1. Multi-Photo Room Gallery

Every luxury room features high-definition photo collections with previous/next carousel controls, photo counters (e.g. Photo 1 of 4), and interactive thumbnail strip navigation.

2. Room Photos QR Code Option

Dedicated "Photos QR" button on each room card. Scanning the dynamic QR code with a smartphone camera opens the room's mobile tour and photo gallery directly.

3. Indian Rupee (₹ INR) & GST

Full Indian Rupee currency standard with 12% hospitality occupancy tax, itemized breakdown, and printable official tax invoice receipts.

4. Verified UPI QR Payment

Supports GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM UPI with verified account parameters and strict 12-digit UPI UTR validation.
